Getting from the airport to the flat

🚆 BER to Prenzlauer Berg by S-Bahn S85 (direct, no change) - RECOMMENDED for Friday midday

€ Cheap
~44 min · 11 min walk at the end
💶 from €10 · for two €5 each, ABC single ticket valid 2 h.

Cheapest, no changes: one train with luggage, you step off 11 min walk from the flat. Same door-to-door time as a taxi at Friday midday.

  1. Land ~12:00; allow ~30-45 min to clear immigration/baggage, then follow 'S-Bahn / Bahnhof' signs: Terminal 1 station is on level U2 directly under the terminal (Terminal 2 arrivals: level E0, follow signs to Terminal 1).
  2. Buy ABC single tickets (EUR 5.00 each) at the machines/BVG app BEFORE going to the platform; paper tickets must be validated (stamped) in the platform boxes.
  3. Take S85 (Gleis 6 in the 12:45 example) towards Frohnau; ride ~44 min via Schoeneweide / Treptower Park / Ostkreuz and the northern Ring.
  4. Alight at S Prenzlauer Allee, exit to Prenzlauer Allee street, walk ~11 min / 792 m to the flat.

🧳 S-Bahn has level boarding and luggage space; 792 m walk with cases on pavements - fine unless bags are very heavy (tram stop Husemannstr. is only 205 m/5 min from the door if you prefer a tram last leg).

BVG planner (v6.bvg.transport.rest, Fri 27 Nov): S85 BER 12:45 -> Prenzlauer Allee 13:29 direct (44 min); reverse 12:07 -> 12:54 (47 min). S9+S42 via Treptower Park is a 1-change alternative (13:26). Re-check Fahrinfo on the day.

🚆 BVG/VBB tickets: what to buy (zone ABC, 2026 prices)

Plan: ABC for the airport trips, AB for round Berlin. Per person pay-as-you-go is about EUR 29-32 for the whole weekend.

  1. BER is zone C: any trip between BER and Prenzlauer Berg needs an ABC ticket (single EUR 5.00, valid 2 h, changes allowed, no return trips).
  2. Within Berlin (A+B) use AB: single EUR 4.00, or 24-hour AB EUR 11.20 (a day with 3+ journeys is cheaper with the 24h card).
  3. Suggested: Fri - 2x ABC single EUR 10 (arrival) then AB 24h ticket(s) for sightseeing; Sun - ABC single for the airport (or 24h ABC EUR 12.90 if you also ride in the morning).
  4. Buy at BVG/S-Bahn machines (stations, BER), in the BVG Ticket-App / VBB / S-Bahn app, or BVG sales points; buses accept cards/Apple Pay/Google Pay (no cash); trams have on-board machines. Paper tickets must be stamped; fine for no ticket EUR 60.
  5. Group 24h ticket (up to 5 people): AB EUR 35.30 / ABC EUR 37.70 - not cheaper for two.

🧳 Buy tickets before heading down to the BER station platforms.

🚆 Berlin WelcomeCard 48h/72h vs ordinary tickets

Only worth it if you will use 2+ paid attractions with 25-50% discounts; ride-only it costs MORE than pay-as-you-go tickets for this itinerary.

  1. Ride-only comparison per person: 72h ABC WelcomeCard EUR 45.50 (valid 72 h from validation, includes BER and all of Berlin) vs ~EUR 29-32 with single/24h tickets.
  2. Museum Island version (from EUR 62, 72 h) gives free entry once a day to the 5 Museum Island museums (excluding special exhibitions) - only if the couple want Pergamon/Neues Museum etc.
  3. WelcomeCard partners: 25-50% discounts at ~170-180 partners (TV Tower, DDR Museum, Madame Tussauds, city tours, theatres).
  4. Verdict: skip it unless the plan includes Museum Island plus other paid sights; otherwise buy ABC single (arrival), AB 24h days, ABC single (departure).

🧳 n/a

72h ABC 45.50 / AB 39.50 confirmed (bvg.de). Card covers 1 adult + up to 3 kids, so 2 adults need 2 cards. 48h price not published on the page. Pay-as-you-go gap is smaller than scout claimed if 3 separate 24h tickets are bought; 4-trip tickets are cheaper.

€€€ Expensive
~45 min
💶 from €60 · typically €70 · up to €75 · for the car Metered; about €65–75 to Prenzlauer Berg including the €1.50 airport surcharge. Ride-hail apps use dynamic prices.

Door-to-door with luggage and no changes; worth it if arrival is delayed, bags are heavy, or a 04:00-06:00 airport run is needed.

  1. Taxi: use the north or south taxi rank in front of T1 on arrivals level E0 (Terminal 2 has no rank - follow footpath to T1). Only use marked taxis from the rank.
  2. Fare: metered; berlin.de lists about EUR 58 BER - Alexanderplatz and about EUR 63 BER - Zoo/Kurfuerstendamm, including EUR 1.50 airport surcharge. Prenzlauer Berg 10437 is a few km north of Alexanderplatz so expect roughly EUR 60-75. Cards usually accepted but drivers are not obliged to accept all cards - carry some cash.
  3. Uber / Bolt / FREENOW: airport has a dedicated ride-app pick-up area in the approach lane in front of T1, level E0; prices are dynamic (no official published fare) - compare in the apps.
  4. Pre-booked transfer: possible (often fixed price, usually a little above the metered fare); only worth it for early-morning departures or if you want a named driver waiting.
  5. Honest recommendation for two adults with bags arriving Fri 12:00: take S85 direct (about 55 min door-to-door, EUR 10 total) - it is nearly as quick as a taxi, not subject to A100 traffic. Use a taxi/ride-hail if flight is late, bags are very heavy, or either of you is tired.

🧳 Standard taxi fits 2 large cases; ride-app 'Comfort/XL' if you have more.

Practical cheat sheet

  • Cold & dark — Forecast is dry but freezing (about -4°C to 0°C all weekend). Sunset is about 16:00 each day, so daylight sightseeing needs to be indoors or done before 15:30. Hat, gloves and a proper coat.
  • Getting around — Buy tickets in the BVG app or at station machines before boarding. Airport trips need an ABC ticket (€5 single, valid 2 h); inside Berlin AB (€4 single, €11.20 for 24 h). Contactless is not a ticket on trains, so validate paper tickets. The U-Bahn runs all night on Fri and Sat nights.
  • Trains to the airport — S9/S85 to BER are closed from Fri 27 Nov 21:30 to Mon 30 Nov 01:30. For the return use the FEX / RE20 from Potsdamer Platz or Hauptbahnhof (U2 from Eberswalder Straße to Potsdamer Platz), or a taxi. Leave the flat about 3 hours before a flight. Check the BVG/VBB app for diversions on the day.
  • Sunday — Sunday is quiet in Germany: ordinary shops are shut, but museums, cafés, restaurants, markets and concerts run. Many restaurants close Sunday or Monday: check the Sun hours on each card.
  • Booking — Book ahead for the tasting-menu restaurants, TV Tower, Museum für Naturkunde time slots, Computerspielemuseum, Spy Museum, Christmas Garden and the big concerts. Everything marked 'Book ahead' has an official link on its card.
  • Spas — Vabali is a textile-free (nude) sauna and spa world, so only book it if that suits you both. Check the house rules of Vabali and Liquidrom on their sites, and book a time slot.
  • Vegan food — Every restaurant here was checked for a real vegan or full vegan menu; where a place is vegetarian rather than fully vegan the card says so. Many jazz clubs and music venues are bars, so expect to be among drinkers.
  • Money — Euro (€). Cards are widely accepted but some smaller cafés, bakeries and markets are cash-only: carry some cash.
  • Entry rules (check before you fly) — UK passport holders: passport issued less than 10 years ago and valid at least 3 months beyond departure. The EU's ETIAS travel authorisation has no confirmed start date yet. Check gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/germany and the EU Entry/Exit System page just before you travel.

Items with a 'Double-check' badge had conflicting or single sources. Items that could not be confirmed were left out. Always look at the venue's own page on the day.
